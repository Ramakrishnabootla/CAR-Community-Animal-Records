THE GAP

What do we already know about them?

Were they already vaccinated? Have they been ill before? Did they move in recently? Have they had surgery? Were they lost, found or abandoned? Are they a rare or fading native type?

Information about community animals exists — but it is scattered across people, organizations, messages, photographs and records.

CAR connects these fragments to one animal and builds a continuing history over time.
